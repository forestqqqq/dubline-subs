最后，视频还会带大家了解 xTerra Robotics 从 IIT Kanpur 研究项目到班加罗尔商业化的历程，以及印度硬件生态在芯片和电池等环节的供应链现状。

## 原视频简介

xTerra Robotics is quickly establishing itself as one of India's most advanced robotics startups, transitioning from an academic research project at IIT Kanpur into a full-fledged commercial enterprise based in Bengaluru. Today, up to 80% of their bill of materials is sourced within India. By creating their own proprietary actuators, custom electronics motherboards, and bespoke passive cooling systems, Aditya Pratap Singh Rajawat and team are solving fundamental engineering challenges related to thermal management, compact volume, and high endurance. This ground-up approach not only allows them to push their hardware to the absolute limits during testing but also positions them to mass-produce highly affordable, rugged robots for global and domestic markets.
