本期视频将带你深入了解当前快速增长的AI工程师岗位趋势。通过引用Quess India等权威报告数据，我们揭示了AI工程作为热门技术领域的现状，并对比分析了全面版与加速版两套实战路线图，旨在帮助你清晰掌握从基础软件工程到LLM应用构建、评估及安全的全栈技能路径。

在视频中，你可以学到以下核心知识点：
1. 明确AI工程师的核心职责，重点掌握RAG（检索增强生成）、Agent（智能体）等高频实战技术；
2. 深入理解上下文窗口限制、推理成本差异以及混合检索与重排序等关键工程细节；

4. 掌握LLM应用评估体系（离线/在线测试）及AI安全防护（红队测试）的实操步骤；
5. 了解LLMOps中的实验追踪、CI/CD流水线及容器化部署等保障系统稳定运行的关键技术。

## 原视频简介

This session breaks down the role of an AI Engineer—focusing on developing, evaluating, and deploying production LLM-based applications rather than training base foundation models from scratch. We analyze real-world hiring trends, demand shifts, and labor market data from the Quess India AI Workforce Report, demonstrating why hands-on expertise in RAG, Agentic AI, and system evaluations outpaces traditional data science demand. You will receive a walkthrough of two learning roadmaps: an exhaustive 16-level, 286-session curriculum covering everything from software engineering fundamentals to system design and LLMOps, alongside a high-velocity 100-day accelerated sprint designed to teach the essential 80% through application-first development.
