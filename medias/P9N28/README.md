## 原视频简介

Yann LeCun has spent years telling the AI industry it's making a huge mistake, and a new paper might be the strongest evidence yet that LeCun was right. In "Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs", Randall Balestriero and Yann LeCun show that a 109 million parameter JEPA beat language models up to 72 billion parameters on how well it lines up with the way image models see the world, with about 42x less training data. In this video I explain what LeCun actually found, in plain English, and what it could mean if this idea is scaled up.
